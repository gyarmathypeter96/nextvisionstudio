The best way to compare video production quotes is to compare their deliverables and exclusions, not only the headline figure. Ask what planning, filming, editing, versions, revisions, travel and usage are included. A lower filming-only quote can be appropriate, but it is a different scope from a finished video package.

## Planning and filming

Confirm shoot hours, crew size, locations, equipment and who supplies the brief or script. Ask if travel, parking, studio hire, additional crew or overtime are included. A session involving several sites has different travel and setup needs from one interview in a single room.

## Editing and delivery

Specify the number and approximate length of finished edits, their orientation, captions and graphics. Ask whether the price covers raw footage, additional versions, audio cleanup and any agreed music. If a deadline is fixed, discuss delivery before booking rather than assuming a rush turnaround is included.

## Revisions and rights

Agree who approves the video, how many rounds of consolidated feedback are included and how changes after approval are priced. Check the permission to use the finished video on your website, social accounts and paid ads. Any music, stock images or other third-party material may have separate licence conditions.

## Compare like with like

Write down each quote as filming time plus the finished assets and extras.
