A ventilation company and a dog grooming business need different videos. Their customers have different questions, and the people on camera should sound like the business the viewer will meet.

For VentSolve, our work includes promotional films, customer education and internal training. At Leroy’s Barking World in Blessington, the brief was to add planned professional videos alongside the owner’s everyday phone content. Both projects started with what the audience needed to understand.

Here are the production decisions behind those two briefs, with links to the public work. These are ongoing projects, not a claim that every example was made in one filming day.

## VentSolve needed separate messages for separate audiences

Someone researching ventilation needs to understand the service before making an enquiry. An existing customer may need a more detailed explanation, while a team member needs instructions they can follow.

The VentSolve partnership therefore includes different types of content:

* Promotional videos introducing the business and its services.
* Educational videos explaining ventilation and mould removal.
* Internal videos for team training.
* Short social edits around individual messages and campaigns.

Our job was to turn technical knowledge into talking points and visual explanations. A promotional edit can introduce a service quickly. An educational video needs enough time to explain it accurately. Internal training has its own purpose and is not published as a marketing example.

You can [watch the VentSolve brand film and public social examples](/case-studies/ventsolve-content-production/). The case study describes the wider partnership separately from the work available to view.

### What another service business can take from this

Bring the questions you answer on sales calls. Choose the question that most often stops a customer understanding your offer, then show the part of your work that answers it.

For example, a planned service explanation could cover who the service suits, what happens during the work and what the customer should do next. That is a suggested structure for a new brief, not a transcript or an additional claim about the VentSolve project.

Our [business and corporate video production in Dublin](/corporate-video-production-dublin/) covers this kind of service explanation, as well as larger training or interview briefs.

## Leroy’s Barking World needed to keep its personality

The dog grooming business already had phone videos showing daily life. Professional production needed to work alongside those videos and keep the people and routines recognisable.

We prepared scripts, ideas and a shot plan before filming. The direction combined funny, educational dog grooming content with clearer shots and editing. Preparing what to say also gave the client support while building confidence on camera.

That balance matters for a local business. The finished video should help a new customer understand the business they are actually going to visit.

### What another local business can take from this

List the routines customers ask about and the people they will meet. A filming plan can turn those subjects into professional short videos while leaving room for spontaneous phone posts between shoots.

A [planned Content Day](/short-form-video-production-dublin/) fits a defined batch of short videos. [Monthly social media content](/social-media-content-creation-dublin/) suits a business that needs fresh material on an ongoing basis. The amount of content and any publishing support should be agreed in the proposal.

## Decide what the viewer should do after watching

A service video can sit beside the relevant website enquiry form. A short social video may ask the viewer to visit the service page or send a message. Give each video a next step that matches what it explains. Our guide to [planning a business video production](/blog/business-video-production-planning-dublin/) shows how to write that step into the brief before filming.

Before quoting a new project, we need to know where the content will be used, who can appear and the date it is needed. That determines the filming plan and edits more usefully than choosing a camera first.
