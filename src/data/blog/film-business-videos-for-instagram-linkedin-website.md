One shoot can support several channels when the formats and messages are planned before filming. List where each finished video will appear, who will watch it and what they need to do. Agree the number of versions and aspect ratios in the quote; a cropped copy is not automatically a well-composed edit for every screen.

## Choose the main use first

A website explainer may need context about a service and a clear route to an enquiry. A short social video may answer one question or show one detail. LinkedIn content can introduce the people or decisions behind the work. These are possible uses, not rules that fit every audience.

## Frame for the formats you actually need

Vertical videos suit many mobile placements while a website player may favour a horizontal frame. Ask the producer whether a shot will be recorded separately for each format or framed with enough room for a later crop. Important faces, demonstrations and on-screen text must remain visible in each version.

## Plan captions and sound

Many people encounter videos without immediately turning on sound. Accurate captions help them follow speech, but review spelling, names and technical terminology. Record clear audio even when captions are planned: a usable soundtrack also helps viewers who listen.

## Agree the edit list

Specify the number, duration and orientation of the edits, caption style, music rights and review rounds. If a video will be used in paid advertising, check usage rights and whether an advertising-specific version is needed.
