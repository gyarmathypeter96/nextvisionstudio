A half-day video shoot is a defined filming session, not a promise of unlimited finished videos. Before booking, agree the actual hours, location, people, filming plan and the edits you need. At NextVision, the published half-day shooting rate is €250; editing is quoted separately. The written quote confirms the scope and any extras.

## What happens during a half-day shoot?

A practical session can include setup, a short sound and lighting check, interviews or direct-to-camera answers, and footage of the work. The balance depends on your location and brief. Setup, moving rooms and resetting a demonstration take time, so prioritise the material that cannot be filmed later. Confirm whether travel and setup count within the booked hours.

## What is included after filming?

Filming and editing are different stages. Ask whether the quoted fee includes selecting footage, cutting finished videos, captions, music licensing, colour work, formats and revision rounds. If you need several social clips, agree the number and scope of edits in writing. A filming-only rate should not be compared with a package that includes planning and edited videos.

## How should a business prepare?

Choose one audience and a short list of questions. Confirm who will be on camera, book a quiet room and gather any products or equipment needed for a demonstration. Share accessibility, parking and site rules in advance. A short shot list should identify must-have footage, followed by optional material if time allows.

## When is a longer shoot a better choice?

Multiple locations, a live event schedule, additional speakers or complex lighting can make a full day more suitable. A single focused interview may need less time. Discuss the brief before choosing a duration; the right answer depends on the work, not a fixed video count.
