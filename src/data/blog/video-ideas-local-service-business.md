Useful local business videos answer the questions a customer asks before getting in touch: what you do, who it helps, what happens during a visit and how to book. Start with a real question from your team, choose one clear answer and show the relevant work. You can film several related answers during one organised visit.

## 1. Explain what happens first

Walk through the first appointment or enquiry. Show the setting or preparation and explain what the customer needs to provide. Avoid promising a time or outcome that varies by job.

## 2. Answer a common concern

Choose a genuine question about access, preparation, turnaround or what is included. State when the answer depends on an assessment. A clear qualification builds more trust than an exaggerated guarantee.

## 3. Show the work behind the result

Film a small part of the process and explain why it matters. Avoid exposing private customer details or filming unsafe work purely for a dramatic shot. Confirm permission before showing a customer location.

## 4. Introduce the person doing the work

Let a team member explain a part of their role in their own words. The [Perfect Clean project](/case-studies/perfect-clean-video-advertising/) pairs the owner speaking with the work process. It shows an approach to a service message, not a claim about campaign results.

## 5. Show the right fit; 6. explain the next step; 7. revisit a seasonal question

Help viewers recognise when the service is relevant and when they should ask for an assessment. Explain how to contact the business and what information is helpful. For seasonal topics, update the advice when the actual service or timing changes; do not invent urgency.

## Turn ideas into a useful series

Write the audience, question, answer and supporting footage for each idea. A single planned session can capture a batch, with the number of edits and approvals agreed beforehand.
