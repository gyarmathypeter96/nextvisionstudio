A customer journey can start with a useful video and continue through a website enquiry and a timely response. Video production, advertising and CRM setup are separate services with different deliverables. Before connecting them, decide what the customer needs at each step and who is responsible for responding.

## Begin with a real customer question

Choose a question your business can answer accurately. Film the people or process that makes the answer clearer. The [Perfect Clean project](/case-studies/perfect-clean-video-advertising/) shows an owner-led advertising creative and work process; its case study does not claim campaign results.

## Send interested people somewhere relevant

A website page should explain the specific service, relevant conditions and how to get in touch. Paid advertising needs its own audience, budget and measurement plan. Producing a video does not by itself include advertising management or guarantee leads.

## Give the team a clear next step

A CRM can record enquiries, ownership and follow-up dates. Agree who responds, what information is required and how a customer can opt out of communication. Automation should support the team with useful reminders and an agreed process rather than send messages blindly.

## Check the complete path

Review the form, confirmation and actual enquiry receipt before relying on a campaign. Measure the journey with consent-aware tools, then compare qualified enquiries and booked work where the data is available. A click or a form event is not automatically a paying customer.
