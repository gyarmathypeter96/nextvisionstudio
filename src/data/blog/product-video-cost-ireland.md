A product video is priced around what must be demonstrated, how the product is filmed and how many finished edits you need. NextVision’s published **4 short videos for ads package is €500 + VAT**. That is a defined advertising-video package, not a fixed price for every product film. A detailed demonstration or larger product range needs its own quote.

## Decide what the customer needs to understand

A product page may need a clear demonstration of size, setup or use. An advert may need a short opening built around a particular benefit. A brand film may concentrate on materials, atmosphere or how the product is made.

Choose the job before choosing the length. If a buyer cannot see how a product works from photographs, show that process. If the goal is a paid campaign, agree the hook, offer and next action. ## The production choices that affect the price

**Product preparation:** how many products and variants are ready to film? Bring clean final samples, working components and backups where appropriate. Packaging, fingerprints and changing labels can add avoidable work.

**Demonstration:** a simple tabletop detail shot needs a different setup from a product being installed, worn or used in a real setting. Tell us if hands, a presenter, customers or specialist instructions are needed.

**Background and movement:** controlled lighting, a location change or a sequence showing several steps changes the shoot plan. A brief may need both clean detail shots and wider context.

**Versions:** specify the number of videos, aspect ratios, captions, graphics and advertising variants. Changing the opening of an advert is still editing work even when the central footage is shared.

## Get comparable quotes for your product video

List the product count, demonstration steps, location, formats and deadline. Supply links to the product pages and any usage instructions the video must follow. Ask each producer to quote the same finished deliverables, music usage and revision scope.
