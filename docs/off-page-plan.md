## 1. Client footer links

Ask first. Place it in the footer, not hidden.

VentSolve (WordPress, ventsolve.ie): Appearance → Widgets → Footer, or the
theme footer builder. Add a Custom HTML block:

```html
<p class="site-credit">Website &amp; video by <a href="https://www.nextvisionstudio.com/case-studies/ventsolve-content-production/">NextVision Studio</a></p>
```

Perfect Clean (perfectclean.ie):

```html
<p class="site-credit">Website &amp; video by <a href="https://www.nextvisionstudio.com/webdesigner-dublin/">NextVision Studio</a></p>
```

Use a normal link (no `nofollow`) only where NextVision actually built the
site. If it only produced video, link the matching case study and use "Video by
NextVision Studio".

that names the service; this helps the profile show for those searches.

Email:

> Subject: Quick favour – your feedback on the [service] project
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks again for working with us on [project]. If you were happy with the
> result, would you leave a short Google review? A sentence on what we made
> (for example the [product video / photos / website]) and how it helped your
> business in Dublin would mean a lot.
>
> https://g.page/r/CeqNk_hiVaxOEBM/review
>
> Thank you,
> Peter – NextVision Studio

WhatsApp:

> Hi [Name], thanks again for the [service] project! If you were happy with it,
> could you leave a quick Google review? One or two sentences on what we did is
> perfect: https://g.page/r/CeqNk_hiVaxOEBM/review 🙏

Never ask friends or family for reviews, and do not offer anything in return.
Google can remove reviews like that.
